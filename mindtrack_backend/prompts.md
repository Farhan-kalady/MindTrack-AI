## 1. Project Overview

MindTrack AI uses Artificial Intelligence to analyze journal entries and provide
emotional wellness insights. The AI detects emotions, scores mood, and gives
personalized suggestions to help users improve their mental well-being.

---

## 2. AI Feature: Emotion Analysis

### Purpose
Analyze journal entries to detect emotions, sentiment, mood score,
and provide supportive wellness suggestions.
